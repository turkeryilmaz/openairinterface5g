How to Read the Configuration

PRB means Physical Resource Block. A resource grid spans PRBs × 12 × subcarrier spacing; a nominal channel also includes guard bands. Channel-width labels use exact supported PRB/spacing pairs from OAI's FR1 bandwidth table. Other combinations remain unavailable. The radio's analogue filter bandwidth and a UE's active bandwidth part are separate settings.

SSB is the Synchronization Signal Block; its reference frequency identifies the synchronization block. Point A is the frequency reference for the common resource-block grid. Neither generally equals carrier centre frequency. Centre frequencies here come from startup observations. Saved config includes may be absent; recorded scalar CLI overrides take priority.

Antenna ports describe signal-processing and reference-signal relationships. RF channels are active hardware streams. Neither proves the physical antenna-element or cable count. FDD means Frequency Division Duplex, with separate UL and DL frequencies; TDD means Time Division Duplex, using a time pattern. TDD slot/symbol counts are shown only when saved.

HARQ means Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request. Attempt limits include the first transmission. Round 0 is the initial attempt; round 1 is the first retry. Use the recorded limit, which can vary by direction and capture.
